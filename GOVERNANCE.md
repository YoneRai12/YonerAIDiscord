# Governance / ガバナンス

日本語: この配布物の保守判断は権利者または指定maintainerが行います。提案、Issue、PRは検討対象ですが、採用や期限を保証しません。安全、公開境界、非商用条件に反する変更は拒否されます。

English: The rights holder or designated maintainers make stewardship decisions for this release. Proposals, issues, and pull requests may be considered, but acceptance and timing are not guaranteed. Changes that conflict with safety, the public boundary, or non-commercial terms will be declined.
